# Recommended PPU v0.1 Purchasing-Power Standard
WP1A • 2026-09-19 • Proposed specification for Gate 1A; not ratified

## Proposed economic promise
**One PPU is intended to have a U.S.-dollar reference value that tracks cumulative changes in the U.S. urban consumer price level, as measured by the BLS CPI-U, U.S. City Average, All Items, Not Seasonally Adjusted, relative to a defined base reference.**

Underlying series identity: **CUUR0000SA0**, published index reference base **1982–1984 = 100**. This statistical base is not the PPU genesis/base date, which is unselected. [BLS identifier](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/cpi-series-ids.htm), [BLS reference-base explanation](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/rebasing.htm).

This is a proposed benchmark standard. Final target timing, base reference, vintage and exceptional-state rules remain for WP1B. It is not a guarantee of any individual's living costs, a fixed physical basket, constant welfare, asset affordability, worldwide purchasing power, a current-day price level, or a market price/redeemability outcome. The latter outcomes require later economic, operational and legal validation.

## Choices recommended for review
The recommendation is conditional on approval of the explicit priority rule in the research report: meaningful population fit first, then operational vintage/matching practicality over incremental substitution fidelity. It is not evidence of user demand. The selected series has a documented missing October 2025 observation and subsequent compilation effects; its historical continuity must not be overstated.
1. Adopt the named CPI-U NSA all-items U.S. City Average as the underlying v0.1 benchmark, conditional on accepting the stated scope and limitations.
2. Use “U.S. urban consumer-price-indexed” as the precise description; qualify any broader purchasing-power shorthand.
3. Keep v0.1's benchmark U.S.-based even if transferable globally. Make geographic and currency basis explicit.
4. Defer proprietary index research; exclude a proprietary controlling index from v0.1 on current evidence. Retain C-CPI-U, PCE and faster private observations as research comparators, not selected fallback indexes.
5. Carry the TIPS reference convention forward as a WP1B candidate, with its costs and institutional dependencies exposed. **Do not adopt its lag, rounding, deflation, vintage, extrapolation or successor rules at Gate 1A.**

Rationale and disconfirming evidence: [research report](01-purchasing-power-standard-research-report.md), [benchmark matrix](02-comparative-benchmark-matrix.md). This recommendation narrows H001; it does not validate H002/H003 or solve any capital question.

## Preconditions for an operational specification — later work, not current approval requests
WP1B must specify a complete deterministic reference calculation and handle correction, missing observations, prolonged outage, methodology evolution and deflation. It must quantify acceptable lag exposure and distinguish target continuity from authentic observation. WP1D/Phase 2 must test match and liquidity. WP1G must confront external discretion and protected terms. User-facing promise validation remains an open question; this report is not market-demand evidence.
