## Evidence handling and uncertainty
Facts in reports cite publisher URLs near their use; judgments and illustrative equations are identified separately. No historical returns/dataset was downloaded or validated. No error-adjusted C-CPI/PCE contract or proprietary-index audit was built. Pending questions are explicit rather than filled with assumptions. Independent review is an opinion layer; it does not upgrade source facts or approve recommendations.
