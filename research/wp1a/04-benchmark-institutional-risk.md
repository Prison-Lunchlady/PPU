# Benchmark Manipulation / Governance Risk Analysis
WP1A • 2026-09-19 • Benchmark institutions only; no PPU governance design

## Established evidence
BLS methodology openly includes sampling, quality adjustment, aggregation and expenditure updates. Publication of methods makes those choices inspectable but does not eliminate judgment or guarantee methodological invariance. [Calculation handbook](https://www.bls.gov/opub/hom/cpi/calculation.htm). Housing inflation measures consumption services, including owners' equivalent rent, rather than house purchase prices; this creates an asset-affordability mismatch by design, not evidence of falsification. [BLS housing factsheet](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/owners-equivalent-rent-and-rent.htm).

The 2025 shutdown interrupted collection and created missing inputs, with later implications for expenditure weighting. Thus funding and institutional continuity affect statistics even without a change to the nominal index name. [BLS impact FAQ](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/additional-resources/2025-federal-government-shutdown-impact-cpi-faq.htm), [missing Consumer Expenditure panels](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/notices/2026/missing-ce-data-panels.htm).

Treasury's existing terms permit replacement of an index judged materially adversely altered and specify handling when observations are late. This demonstrates externally retained judgment in the hedge's contract, not an immutable basket guarantee. [Treasury terms, Appendix B, I.B.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-356).

## Threat analysis — interpretation, not allegations
| Failure/influence channel | Consequence for PPU research | Residual uncertainty / later route |
|---|---|---|
| Legitimate methodological evolution | Same series name can represent evolving weights/quality conventions | Materiality and monitoring requirements in WP1B/1G; no automatic freeze or replacement chosen |
| Political pressure or resource control | Possible delays, impaired collection or changed measurement incentives | No probability or deliberate-falsification finding established; institution risk cannot be coded away |
| Missing/corrected observations | Two honest data feeds can disagree across vintages | Define binding input rules in WP1B and verify later oracle requirements |
| Confidential input data | Independent observers cannot completely reconstruct the statistic from public raw inputs | Separate published-value verification from measurement trust; disclose R022 |
| Multi-provider delivery of one BLS number | Improves availability, not independent economic measurement | Do not count mirrors as distinct statistical institutions; Phase 4 dependency |
| Private provider data/license/method changes | Changes access, composition or continuity | Independent audit, full methodology/vintage access and exit/continuity evidence needed before reconsidering |
| Proprietary PPU basket | PPU itself gains influence over what counts as inflation | Defer controlling-index proposal; research only if measurable benefit exceeds institution-building costs |
| Mechanical following of Treasury successor | Potentially imports outside judgment into protected PPU promise | Explicit unresolved protected-target versus hedge-continuity tension, WP1B/1G |

**Recommendation:** describe the index as externally administered and publicly documented, not manipulation-proof or independent of governments. A benchmark cannot both keep adapting to contemporary consumption and preserve every methodological parameter forever. This is a choice requiring transparent requirements and review, not a reason to secretly choose a successor rule in WP1A.

**Unresolved uncertainty:** no complete audit of institutional independence, dataset licensing or private-provider operations was conducted. Public methodology documents cannot prove absence of hidden influence. The CPI-U recommendation therefore accepts an explicit measurement institution dependency; if that dependency violates the intended product promise, the promise or benchmark must be reconsidered before approval.

## Preserved tensions
The earlier conflicts about insolvency versus claim protection, emergency restrictions versus orderly redemption, index contingency versus protected terms, and voluntary version migration versus reserve/custody continuity remain unresolved. This analysis adds evidence about the index conflict; it does not settle any of them. No PPU voting system, emergency authority, appointment process or governance constitution is designed here.
