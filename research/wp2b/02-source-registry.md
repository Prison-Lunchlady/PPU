# WP2B Source Registry

Date: 2026-09-30.

**W2B-S01 — BLS CPI-U NSA.** Direct BLS flat-file series CUUR0000SA0 plus BLS CPI technical/seasonal-adjustment documentation. Primary source. The protocol benchmark is unadjusted CPI; current published history is not treated as proof of every original release vintage.

**W2B-S02 — Federal Reserve H.15.** Federal Reserve Board Selected Interest Rates and Data Download Program. Primary source for nominal Treasury constant maturities, Treasury bills, and inflation-indexed Treasury constant-maturity yields.

**W2B-S03 — FRED official retrieval layer.** Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis graph-download endpoints are an official Federal Reserve distribution path for Board/New York Fed source series. The originating source remains recorded.

**W2B-S04 — New York Fed reference rates.** SOFR publication/methodology and Markets Data API material. Primary source. Reference-rate terms and disclaimers remain attached to use.

**W2B-S05 — Chicago Fed NFCI.** Candidate weekly financial-conditions indicator extending to 1971. Chicago Fed states its history may change as incoming data, revisions, and estimated weights change. FRED identifies the series as copyrighted/citation-required, so raw NFCI values are not vendored into this public repository until redistribution treatment is confirmed.

**W2B-S06 — EIA WTI Cushing.** Energy-stress candidate. EIA provides long historical spot-price tables, but some wholesale spot-price inputs are republished with permission. Raw public-repository redistribution requires a source/terms check before vendoring.

**W2B-S07 — World Bank Pink Sheet.** Commodity-price research candidate. Exact raw-series license/provenance must be confirmed before committing data.

**W2B-S08 — Gold, equity and Bitcoin market prices.** Candidate histories frequently rely on third-party exchanges/index providers. WP2B does not copy a convenient series into the public repository without sufficient provenance and redistribution rights.

## Priority rule

Primary public authority > official Federal Reserve distribution > clearly licensed public dataset > third-party market feed. Availability is not evidence of suitability or redistribution permission.
