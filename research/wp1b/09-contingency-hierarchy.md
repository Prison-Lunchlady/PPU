# Index-Contingency and Successor Hierarchy

> **Revision 0.3 controls impairment policy.** Primary disposition: REVISION REQUIRED. Revised submission awaits primary review; no Gate 1B approval. [Benchmark Impairment Policy](18-benchmark-impairment-policy.md) supersedes earlier indefinite-freeze recommendations and impairment-clearance shorthand. The public interface for this proposal is `replay_with_impairment` in [impairment_policy.py](impairment_policy.py), wrapping the unchanged arithmetic model. Other mechanics remain conditional proposals; WP1C is unauthorized.


This is a proposed index-specific authority constraint, not a governance architecture or grant of live power.

1. Valid original observation and normal identity: deterministic admission and daily path.
2. Delay, missing observation, temporary delivery/source failure: immutable committed path, then mechanical endpoint carry; prospective recovery only.
3. Official correction of the relevant first edition: preserve vintage/history, block data health, freeze uncommitted plans pending transparent review.
4. Pure base change: prefer old-base continuation, else exact officially supported normalization; no rounded-pair inference. If equivalence is uncertain, review hold.
5. Routine sampling/weights/quality maintenance within existing framework: disclose and inherit. Material population/coverage/formula/identity change: review hold, even if series ID remains the same.
6. Discontinuation: hold. An official successor is the first *candidate for review*, never an automatic replacement. Same concept and documented overlap/mapping must be demonstrated. Other public indexes are research alternatives; private/proprietary substitutes require reopening benchmark scope. No administrator selects a low-liability index.
7. Failure to establish an acceptable successor: remain frozen with impaired-coverage disclosure. Seek primary Gate 1A reconsideration or a separately authorized version. Do not impose new terms or decide reserve/liquidation mechanics here.

Mechanical matters: month arithmetic, cutoffs, packet-field equality, positive-value checks, age, interpolation, carry, fixed vintages, exact documented base scaling, reporting counters. Human/institutional matters: authentic source evidence, independence of paths, whether a change is substantive, whether official mapping establishes equivalence, future version/holder protections. No honest rule makes these entirely trustless.

Proposed constraint: evidence/reason published within seven days of a review hold, proposed disposition within thirty; failure keeps the hold. Clearance may confirm only restored original benchmark terms and locked first vintages. Substitution or policy change requires explicit primary/Brad approval and prospective version specification, not emergency in-place discretion. Public impact comparison must show consequences in both inflation and deflation, not only lower liability scenarios. Appointment, quorum, appeal, removal, enforceability and migration continuity are Q011/WP1G/Phase 3 dependencies; no personnel or voting design selected.

Concrete counterexample: BLS's announced 2028 geographic sample update is not automatically a new purchasing-power promise. It updates geographic sampling for population shifts. Rejecting all maintenance would freeze an evolving statistical index almost inevitably; ignoring every same-ID change would permit scope drift. Its detailed compatibility still needs monitoring as methodology is finalized. [BLS notice, July 27, 2026](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/notices/2026/geographic-revision.htm).

Unresolved tension explicitly retained: protected monetary terms versus necessary future successor action; voluntary migration versus ongoing reserve obligations; refusal to forecast versus hedge contracts that do. No Gate 1B recommendation silently resolves these.
