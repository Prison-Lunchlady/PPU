All numbers are deliberately adverse synthetic scenarios replacing6 USD million of bills in120 assets, with no baseline bill return in this isolated test. They do **not** estimate historical drawdowns or prove stochastic dominance. Comparative gains also require testing: +25% alternative return contributes1.5; it may help a particular shock but cannot guarantee meeting an arbitrary CPI-linked demand. The scenario suite does not claim a measured diversification premium.

To justify inclusion, future authorized analysis must demonstrate lower joint liability shortfall and deadline-liquidity deficit versus the simplest same-cost Treasury/TIPS baseline across inflation, deflation, rate, run and custody shocks; include transaction costs, tax/legal treatment, market capacity, stressed correlation and operational failures. Historical high return alone is not sufficient. Phase2 should assess alternatives only after its Treasury baseline. Derivatives/leveraged commodity overlays are not smuggled into this unlevered reserve package.

The BIS tokenisation source [S09](11-sources.md) supports the distinction between represented claims and underlying assets/settlement risks. Other rows are financial-mechanism hypotheses and explicit counterexamples, not sourced return estimates. No asset purchase or permanent constitutional exclusion results.

## Joint comparator after stage2
The560 joint cases replace6 initial bill dollars with6 alternative dollars under each of4structures and10background regimes, include2operating-cost dollars and10%incremental sale cost on80%indexed liability demand, and compare the resulting remaining buffer with the same shocked Treasury baseline. Both adverse returns and the additional+25%favorable branch are assumptions, not empirically estimated correlations. The common sale-cost/access assumption may favor the alternative; unknown real access receives no production credit. No alternative is selected or proven dominated across all possible returns/preferences by these signed sensitivity comparisons.

## Final signed sensitivity comparison
The current560joint cases contain both each asset's stated adverse branch and a+25% favorable branch for all4structures/10regimes. Both positive and negative incremental buffers occur; their signs follow assumptions and are not evidence of measured hedge correlation or superiority. The validator checks that both signs are exercised, not that every alternative must fail. Return branches are deliberately transparent counterexamples: they cannot justify automatic inclusion or permanent rejection. Costs enter aggregate outflow funding, not a cash-only deduction that fails when expense exceeds cash. The proposed initial core therefore remains Treasury-based for testing, with no claimed empirical defeat of every alternative.
