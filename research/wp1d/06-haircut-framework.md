# Haircut Framework
Revision0.1 • scenario framework proposed; no production percentages calibrated.

Let inventory market value include all recorded positions and exceptions. Let `Aeligible=sum(free, owned, authenticated, fresh market portions)`. For joint scenario s, `Vreal(s)=sum(free_i*shock_factor_i(s)*(1-execution_discount_i(s)))`; define horizon availability by actual access and settlement. Market assets, stressed realizability and deadline liquidity are separate reports and must NEVER be added. Stale/unknown values produce exceptions, not proof that the true assets disappeared.

Shock factors include only specified mark changes, while incremental execution discounts represent **additional** bid/size/closeout costs not already in the price. An observed distressed bid plus a liquidity-premium decline plus a full stressed bid haircut can triple-count one event. Use one coherent joint path and a reconciliation of each loss component. Correlation must be stressed jointly; no sum of independent normal VaRs as a tail guarantee.

Disclose gross WP1C `L_PPU` without netting. Ratios `Aeligible/L_PPU` and `Vreal/L_PPU` are conditional diagnostics, undefined if L=0 and undetermined if L missing. Other known claims O and incomplete inventory are visible. Full-inventory residual `E=A-L_PPU-O` is an economic estimate, not legally available first-loss capital. For available-pool analysis, remove encumbered assets and only the corresponding covered creditor portion together; residual deficiencies stay in O. Never both remove collateral and subtract its entire secured claim again, or exclude the asset and forget the debt. Whether the allocation is legally valid is unresolved Phase3 evidence, not a netting permission.

Rehyped assets, unverifiable title or inaccessible custodial balances receive zero eligible/horizon credit as applicable; percentage haircuts cannot substitute for proof of title/access. Repo is a receivable supported by collateral, not two assets. Repo margin is a collateral requirement on a counterparty exposure; a PPU reserve haircut is a separate analytical adjustment, not additional cash or capital.

Hypothesis ladder for incremental stress testing, NOT recommended production haircuts: nominal security mark shocks0/-1/-3/-5%; TIPS total-return branches and separate exact rate sensitivities; incremental sale costs0/2/10/20%; counterparty recovery0/50/100% with delayed access. Calibrate with executable size-specific data, maturity and settlement horizon in Phase2. Any increase in haircut can turn yesterday's apparent surplus into a deficiency; no forced sale rule is automatically authorized by that analytical event.

Unbounded or unidentified exposure cannot be cured by an arbitrary large finite haircut. Declare adequacy undetermined, retain the gross obligation and route the missing evidence to its owner. Proposed controls do not establish an accounting standard or legal priority.

## Executable reconciliation after stage2
Each secured position has a unique paired debt identity/amount; a duplicate ID in other claims is rejected. Conditional allocated debt is min(reserved collateral portion,paired debt), with remaining debt retained against the free pool. Stressed allocation uses stressed collateral realizability, not yesterday's mark; missing/stale evidence gives no stressed allocation credit. This is only a declared analytical allocation, not evidence of legal setoff or enforceable lot division.

Outputs distinguish full_inventory_reference_buffer, free_pool_market_buffer, free_pool_stress_buffer and horizon_gap. Full-inventory reference marks can remain visible for exceptions but are not verified economic values. For a complete verified market snapshot, full buffer minus free-pool buffer = reserved asset value minus conditionally allocated secured debt. For exceptions, the full buffer is withheld; raw inventory quantities and known debt remain visible rather than asserting an unverified identity as coverage. This prevents silently omitting deficiency or counting covered debt twice. Aggregate other liabilities require explicit unique IDs; a renamed duplicate or a hidden legal claim still defeats completeness. Horizon gap requires a supplied due-outflow schedule including relevant other debt/expenses; it is not automatically total gross debt.

If part of a pledged lot cannot actually be separated, flag ownership/access evidence invalid for the whole lot; no arithmetic partition grants legal liquidity. The code trusts these input flags, cannot authenticate them and always reports production_activation=False. BENCHMARK_IMPAIRED changes disclosure status, not numerical face or remedy; TEMPORARY_CARRY is a reporting alias for the accepted carried-target condition, not a new WP1B state transition.

## Final conservative correction
For an excepted asset, **both** market and stressed allocated secured-debt credit are zero. Known paired debt persists. Missing evidence defaults to excluded/stale rather than successful authentication. The full-inventory buffer is withheld (null) if completeness/asset evidence fails; raw historical inventory marks remain visible solely for reconciliation. No positive unverified residual is published as a buffer. Explicit test inputs can assert known evidence, but cannot establish its truth. Lot-separability remains a required assumed fact.
