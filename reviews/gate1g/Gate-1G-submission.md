## Gate findings
1. Governance authority is enumerated rather than residual.
2. Core monetary semantics are protected from in-place ordinary/emergency amendment.
3. Emergency authority is risk-reducing, temporary and incapable of mint/target/claim rewriting.
4. Timelocks and voluntary migration create an exit/notice path for non-emergency change.
5. Same-semantics oracle replacement is separated from economic benchmark replacement.
6. Index contingency is constrained but does not pretend to solve Q023.
7. Governance-token voting is not required or assumed.
8. Legal and technical enforceability remain later-phase dependencies.
