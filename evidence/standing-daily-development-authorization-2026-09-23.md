The following actions remain expressly prohibited without Brad's explicit approval:

- launching a production token;
- accepting real customer or investor capital;
- committing project funds;
- signing or executing legal agreements;
- representing the project to regulators;
- making irreversible production deployments.

The daily process must not silently promote hypotheses to facts, ratify draft constitutional provisions, or erase open risks/questions merely to advance the roadmap.
