## Gate 1A standing conditions

1. PPU v0.1 shall use a narrowly defined U.S. benchmark promise.

The approved concept is:

**A U.S.-dollar reference value indexed to cumulative changes in the U.S. urban consumer price level measured by CPI-U NSA, U.S. City Average, All Items.**

Do not represent this as preservation of every holder’s individual purchasing power or as a global cost-of-living guarantee.

2. U.S. benchmark scope is a v0.1 design choice, not a permanent constitutional truth.

Record the possibility of later regional or global benchmark architectures as future research only.

3. Gate 1A approves the underlying benchmark only.

It does NOT yet approve:

- three-month lag;
- daily interpolation;
- specific TIPS Reference CPI mechanics;
- rounding;
- binding timestamps;
- revision treatment;
- missing-observation treatment;
- deflation mechanics;
- successor-index rules;
- index-contingency rules.

Those belong to WP1B.

4. WP1B may reopen Gate 1A if target-calculation research demonstrates that CPI-U cannot support a sufficiently deterministic, robust and constitutionally acceptable monetary target.

5. CPI-U is externally administered.

Do not describe the purchasing-power measurement itself as trustless or decentralized merely because PPU’s monetary rules may be transparent and software-enforced.

6. Preserve Q021 as open unless WP1B or later authorized work directly resolves it.

7. Preserve the distinction between selecting CPI-U for PPU v0.1 and claiming that CPI-U is universally or objectively the best inflation measure.

## Specific adversarial cases

The target architecture must be tested against at least:

### Case A — Normal publication

All expected CPI observations exist and are published on schedule.

### Case B — High inflation

Monthly CPI rises rapidly for an extended period.

### Case C — Deflation

The index declines for multiple consecutive months.

### Case D — Missing monthly observation

Model a scenario analogous to October 2025 where the expected all-items CPI observation does not exist.

### Case E — Publication delay

The source exists eventually, but publication is materially delayed.

### Case F — Correction after publication

An authoritative value previously used by the protocol is subsequently corrected.

### Case G — Extended source outage

No usable CPI observations are published for multiple months.

### Case H — Methodology change

BLS materially changes CPI-U methodology while retaining the same series identity.

### Case I — Series replacement

CPI-U is discontinued and an official successor is introduced.

### Case J — Oracle disagreement

Different oracle paths report inconsistent values.

### Case K — Blockchain or oracle downtime

The source data exist but the protocol cannot receive them at the expected time.

### Case L — Malicious or erroneous input

A clearly abnormal value reaches one oracle source.

For each case, define the exact protocol behavior and identify any remaining discretionary authority.

## Design principles

The preferred architecture should maximize:

- determinism;
- reproducibility;
- contractual clarity;
- resistance to discretionary manipulation;
- target finality;
- graceful failure;
- economic continuity;
- compatibility with later reserve hedging.

Do not maximize smoothness merely for appearance.

Do not introduce forecasting solely to eliminate publication lag unless strong evidence demonstrates that doing so improves the monetary architecture without adding unacceptable discretion or model risk.

When data quality deteriorates, prefer conservative behavior.

New issuance should never become easier because source data are stale, missing or uncertain.

## Retroactivity principle

WP1B must explicitly address whether previously established target values are final.

Strong preference should be given to a system in which holders can know what the binding target was at a given historical timestamp without later revisions rewriting prior monetary obligations.

If an alternative approach is recommended, justify the consequences for contracts, accounting and redemption rights.

## Index contingency

Develop a proposed mechanical hierarchy for:

- temporary publication delay;
- missing observation;
- official correction;
- temporary source outage;
- permanent discontinuation;
- official successor index;
- material methodology change.

Do not allow administrators to opportunistically select whichever index reduces PPU liabilities.

Any unavoidable governance role must be:

- narrowly defined;
- transparent;
- time-limited where possible;
- constrained by objective criteria.
