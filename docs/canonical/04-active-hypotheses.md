## Preserved hypothesis set

These should be explicitly recorded rather than silently treated as facts:

**H-001:** CPI-U NSA is currently the strongest starting benchmark because it is externally administered and has a natural hedge through TIPS.

**H-002:** Daily interpolation is preferable to monthly step changes.

**H-003:** A Treasury/TIPS-dominant reserve can sufficiently hedge PPU's inflation-linked liability without relying heavily on risky assets.

**H-004:** First-loss capital can be structured in a way that simultaneously provides meaningful solvency protection and permits practical supply growth.

**H-005:** A functioning redemption mechanism can keep market price sufficiently close to target without algorithmic market intervention.

**H-006:** PPU can permit monetary supply elasticity without granting any party discretionary issuance authority.

**H-007:** Governance can be minimized sufficiently that the protocol does not simply recreate a small private central bank.

**H-008:** A legally enforceable relationship between token liability and off-chain reserves can be constructed.

None should be promoted to constitutional fact until the relevant Work Package resolves it.

## Recovered-source implications — no promotion to fact
H001/H002: post-analysis restates CPI/TIPS matching and lag preference; source assertions are not fresh verified research.
H003: baseline duration/liquidity tests precede risky asset additions; reserve survival and purchasing-power coverage take priority over returns.
H004: 1:1 minting dilutes a 120% capital ratio; providers must fund the extra reserve layer. Narrow-bank-with-equity language is an economic analogy, not a legal classification. Required capital and retained income sufficiency remain unvalidated. A lower 108–112% range appears only as an illustrative possibility.
H005/H006: Article XXII excludes arbitrary supply targets, scheduled/mining emissions, automatic fixed-supply founder allocations and price-only algorithmic expansion; mint/redemption remains the hypothesized mechanism.
H007: Articles XVIII–XXI add voluntary migration, protected-rule prohibitions, expiring risk-reducing emergency powers and objective index fallback; precise implementation and compatibility remain open.
H008: governance protection of claims does not establish enforceability, resolution rights or legal classification. All H001–H008 remain OPEN / UNVALIDATED; no duplicative IDs added.
