# Active Hypothesis Addendum — WP1G

## H-007 — Governance can be minimized sufficiently that the protocol does not simply recreate a small private central bank.
Status: CONDITIONALLY SUPPORTED / NOT VALIDATED.

WP1G defines a coherent capability-limited architecture: protected G0 invariants, bounded and timelocked normal powers, temporary risk-reducing emergency powers, cancellation-only guardian authority, voluntary migration and constrained index contingency.

Supporting evidence is architectural, not operational. H007 remains falsifiable until Phase 3/4 establish legal authority, signer/governance design, code-level capability isolation, timelock enforcement, bypass resistance and incident recovery.

Falsify or materially qualify H007 if any single ordinary authority can change target, liability or supply semantics; emergency powers can increase monetary risk; migration becomes coercive; or provider/upgrade paths bypass G0.
