# Decision Register Addendum — WP1E through WP1G
Controlling with 02-decision-register.md and 02a-gate1d-decision-addendum.md.

## PPU-D020 — WP1E capital structure baseline
Date: 2026-09-25 checkpoint, reconciled 2026-09-27.
Status: ACCEPTED FOR CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT WITH CONDITIONS.
Decision: use a fully funded perpetual common-residual/equity-like junior layer as the research baseline; never double-count capital as a second asset pool; no fixed/cumulative junior coupon or holder put; distributions/repurchases only from verified NORMAL state; no production ratio selected.
Reconsider if Phase 2 shows unsustainable funding/carry or Phase 3 defeats legal priority.

## PPU-D021 — WP1F mint/redemption baseline
Date: 2026-09-26 checkpoint, reconciled 2026-09-27.
Status: ACCEPTED FOR CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT WITH CONDITIONS.
Decision: multiple qualified nonexclusive APs; cash-first final-settlement mint; indexed-until-final-discharge redemption; same-day/T+1 normal service model; formulaic bounded fees; mint disabled outside safe states; no automatic 24/7 reserve-layer promise.
Reconsider if Phase 2/3 shows arbitrage, settlement, legal-right or run-resilience failure.

## PPU-D022 — Minimized governance classification
Date: 2026-09-27.
Status: ACCEPTED FOR CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT WITH CONDITIONS.
Decision: classify governance into G0 protected invariants, G1 bounded parameters, G2 provider/implementation maintenance, G3 temporary risk-reducing emergency authority and G4 constrained index contingency. Material economic changes require a new version and voluntary migration.
Rationale: a role-based capability model narrows governance attack surface better than open-ended discretionary authority or governance-token monetary control.
Reconsider if Phase 3/4 cannot enforce separation or bypass resistance.
