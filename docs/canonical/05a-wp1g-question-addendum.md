# Unresolved Questions Addendum — WP1G

## Q-011 — Governance boundaries
Status: RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE ANSWERED / PRODUCTION IMPLEMENTATION OPEN.
WP1G defines protected rules, adjustable classes, emergency limits, voluntary migration and index-contingency boundaries. Exact legal and technical implementation remains Phase 3/4.

## Q-023 — Structural benchmark failure remedy
Status: OPEN — PRODUCTION-ACTIVATION BLOCKER.
WP1G constrains who may act but does not create the missing economic remedy. A materially different benchmark requires a new version; permanent holder treatment remains unresolved.

## Q-029 — Production governance body
OPEN. What legal entity, committee or multisignature structure, signer independence, quorum and removal process can implement the authority domains without concentration or paralysis? Phase 3/4.

## Q-030 — Timelock and emergency-duration calibration
OPEN. Are the 7-day, 14-day, 30-day, 72-hour and 24-hour research baselines appropriate under realistic security and operational scenarios? Phase 4/adversarial testing.

## Q-031 — Legally compelled account controls
OPEN. How can legally required account restrictions or directives be implemented without turning governance into a general discretionary confiscation or monetary-policy authority? Phase 3.

## Q-032 — Legacy-version servicing
OPEN. How long and under what funding/operational rules must an old version remain supported after a voluntary migration path exists, and when may it enter orderly resolution or wind-down? Phase 2/3/4.

Q021, Q022, Q024, Q025, Q026, Q027 and Q028 remain open at their prior scopes.
