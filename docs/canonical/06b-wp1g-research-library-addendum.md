# Research Library Addendum — WP1G

## SRC021 — FSB Global Stablecoin Recommendations
Financial Stability Board, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Global Stablecoin Arrangements, final report, 2023.
Use: design context for clear governance responsibility and accountability, risk management, recovery/resolution and disclosure.
Limitation: does not establish PPU legal classification.
Source: FSB official publication.

## SRC022 — CPMI-IOSCO PFMI governance principles
Bank for International Settlements / CPMI-IOSCO, Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures and stablecoin-application guidance.
Use: design context for governance clarity, risk accountability and crisis decision procedures.
Limitation: PPU is not classified here as an FMI.
Source: BIS/CPMI-IOSCO official publications.

## SRC023 — OpenZeppelin governance and timelock documentation
OpenZeppelin Contracts governance and access-control documentation.
Use: implementation reality check for timelocks, separated proposer/executor/canceller roles and delayed administration.
Limitation: no Phase 4 chain/library selection is made by citing these patterns.
Source: OpenZeppelin official documentation.
